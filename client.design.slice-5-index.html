“Today” is Saturday 10 Oct 2026, as in slices 3 and 4. Releases, proposals and confirmations carry across the three portals when the design folder is served from one address.

The demo path

  1. Open VAC-1041 (Released to Agencies). Its Candidate proposals lists TechForce Recruitment’s and Nexus Talent’s candidates.
  2. Review Daniel Osei’s proposal: start the review, then accept him on the lowest open position. The booking opens.
  3. Open the week of 5 Oct of BK-2210, Kofi Mensah’s agency-supplied booking: Wednesday is Requested (awaiting agency); ask for Thursday’s confirmation. The week of 28 Sep shows TechForce’s reason on a Justified day.
  4. Open Candidates: everyone booked to Acme, filter by agency, compliance or job role.
  5. Choose Released to Agencies in the Vacancies status filter.

IDs refer to the slice 5 design brief, Part B. P1 to P4 = the demo path. Where the brief and the TSD differ, the pages follow the TSD. A client page shows the charge rate and never a pay figure or the booking’s source; the supplying party is the agency’s name, or Agency when Abstract has turned the client’s setting off (the agencyhidden states).
