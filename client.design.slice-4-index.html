The demo path

  1. Open this week’s grid. Select the rows, choose Fill week, review the preview, then save and confirm.
  2. Submit Marek Nowak’s week (Leeds Hub: approved at submission) and Nadia Hussain’s (Birmingham DC: waits for Abstract).
  3. Open Tasha Williams’s week: held while two days wait for Abstract to price them, with the notification in the bell.
  4. Open a booking’s detail page and its Timesheets panel.
  5. Continue in the Abstract portal’s slice 4 index: price, approve, reopen.

IDs refer to the slice 4 design brief: A = no design before, C = correction to an existing export. P1 = the demo path. Where the brief and the rulings of 8 Nov 2026 differ, the pages follow the rulings.
